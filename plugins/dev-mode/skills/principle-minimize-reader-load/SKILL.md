# Minimize Reader Load

Write so the reader gets the point with the least effort.

**Why:** the reader's time costs more than the writer's. Unread text has no effect.

**Pattern:** point first, then detail they can stop at (per `writing`). Cut anything they don't need to act or decide.

**Anti-patterns:** a recap of steps taken; a wall of caveats; the answer buried in paragraph three.
