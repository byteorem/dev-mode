# writing

Rules have stable IDs so other skills can cite them ("per W3").

## Structure
- **W1.** Lead with the point. The first sentence is the one to keep if the rest is cut.
- **W2.** Layer detail after it. The reader can stop at any layer and still be right.
- **W3.** One idea per paragraph or bullet.
- **W4.** Cut anything the reader doesn't need to act or decide.

## Sentences
- **W5.** Short declarative sentences. Present tense.
- **W6.** No long dashes. Use a period or a comma.
- **W7.** No mid-sentence colons. Labels before a list are fine.
- **W8.** Concrete over abstract: name the file, the number, the page.

## Cut on sight
- **W9.** Filler and preamble: "It's worth noting", "In order to", "Let's dive in".
- **W10.** Hype and hedging: "robust", "seamless", "comprehensive", "just", "simply".
- **W11.** Restating what was just said, or summarizing at the end of something short.
- **W12.** Narrating the document itself: "This PR aims to...".

Run over a draft, then reread it once as the reader would.
